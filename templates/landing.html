The problem

Site monitoring today is manual, slow, and subjective.

Quantity surveys rely on tape, level, and spreadsheets. Progress is estimated by eye, volumes are approximated, and reporting lags weeks behind reality — costly on highway-scale earthwork where a single layer spans kilometres.

Weeks of lag

Manual measurement and reconciliation delay every progress claim.

Approximate volumes

Cross-section estimates miss the true shape of cut and fill.

Subjective calls

"Which layer, how far along?" is judged by eye, not measured.

The methodology

From flight to costed progress, automatically

Seven stages take a pair of drone surveys and return objective, geo-accurate numbers.

  1. STEP 01

    Drone data capture

    High-overlap aerial imagery of the site is flown before and after a construction interval.

  2. STEP 02

    Photogrammetry & digital twin

    WebODM / OpenDroneMap reconstructs a 3D point cloud, DSM, and orthomosaic — a true digital twin of the site.

  3. STEP 03

    Digital Surface Model

    A geo-referenced elevation surface captures the exact terrain height at every point of each survey.

  4. STEP 04

    Earthwork volume (cut / fill)

    A geo-aware calculation differences the before/after DSMs to measure moved material in m³, with a Z-offset datum correction.

  5. STEP 05

    AI road-layer detection

    A custom YOLO26 segmentation model identifies the construction stage — Sub Base, Aggregate Base Course, or Asphalt — at mAP50 ≈ 0.96.

  6. STEP 06

    Bill of Quantities

    Measured volume is multiplied by editable PKR unit rates per detected layer to produce a costed BOQ.

  7. STEP 07

    S-curve progress & report

    Planned vs. actual spend is tracked on an S-curve, and the full analysis exports to an Excel report.

Capabilities

Everything the pipeline delivers

Digital-twin reconstruction

Point cloud, DSM, and orthomosaic from raw imagery — the site, rebuilt in 3D.

AI road-layer detection

A trained YOLO26 segmentation model reads the construction stage from the imagery.

Geo-accurate volumes

Cut/fill differenced on a common grid with a vertical datum correction — measured, not estimated.

Automated BOQ

Editable PKR rates per layer turn volume into a line-item bill of quantities.

S-curve tracking

Planned vs. actual spend with a performance index across survey time-points.

One-click Excel report

Summary, BOQ, and S-curve exported to a shareable workbook.
